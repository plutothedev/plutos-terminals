Why this isn't hype

The mechanics, not the marketing

Three concrete reasons this beats one-Claude-chat for real work. No magic, no LLM-router middleware, just smarter context engineering.

2s

systemPrompt auto-typed

Every pack ships a per-tab systemPrompt. The app spawns Claude, waits 2 seconds for the REPL to be ready, then types the prompt as your first message. No copy-paste, no role drift.

11

shipped + reusable

11 packs in the binary today. Every layout you build can be exported to a .deck.json with one click and shared via gist, GitHub raw, or drag-and-drop. Yesterday's setup, today's session, no re-typing.

∞

parallel terminals, not chats

Each terminal is a real PTY shell with its own Claude. Activity dots (yellow = working, green = done) show what's ready without you flipping tabs. You glance at the grid, you know your pipeline.

10×3

10 skins · 3 densities · ∞ windows

Every visible surface is themed by the active skin (header, sidebar, status bar, terminal background, modals). 10 skins × 3 densities × N windows. The app gets out of your way.
