⭐ `prompt-engineer-lab` is the audience-perfect one — two panes (tutor + clean test bench) that let you iterate on Claude/GPT/agent prompts in real time. The pack BECOMES the demo for AI-curious viewers.

Highlights:
- **`pros-vs-cons`** — three Claudes (PRO / CON / SYNTH). PRO and CON each argue ONE side without hedging; SYNTH finds the load-bearing assumption underneath. Decision-making framework, not a vibe.
- **`research-assistant`** — researcher pane that surfaces 3-5 high-signal sources with citations + scratch for synthesis notes. Pairs with Brave Search MCP if you want live searches.
- **`code-review`** — senior reviewer with three severities (BLOCKER / IMPORTANT / NIT), no praise filler. Cuts the time you spend on every PR.
- **`landing-page-writer`** — won't draft until you answer 5 questions. Forces you to actually have a value prop.
- **`prd-writer`** — same shape: pulls out problem / user / metric / scope cut before drafting.
- **`journal-buddy`** + **`weekly-retro`** — pair them: 5-min daily, 15-min weekly. Pattern-noticed field across 8 weeks beats any single retro.
- **`trip-planner`** + **`meal-planner`** — life utility, makes the app feel personal not just "a coder tool."

All 15 follow the v0.1.23 quality bar: open with first question (no introductions), 3-5 question intake to anchor scope, concrete anti-patterns, specific tone calibration.

### 🔔 Audio cue on activity-done

When a backgrounded agent transitions from working (yellow) to done (green), the app plays a soft two-note ascending pluck. Web-Audio-generated (no asset bundle, no permission prompt), 240ms, won't fire for the tab you're currently viewing.

Implementation: lazy-init `AudioContext` on first transition, two `OscillatorNode`s (660 Hz → 880 Hz), short attack/release envelope. Silent failure if audio is blocked.

**Why:** during multi-agent recordings, viewers HEAR the work finishing — not just see it. Powerful proof-of-parallelism cue.

### 🟢 Bigger activity dots

Activity dots in the tab strip went from **7px to 10px**. Glow size also boosted (8-10px instead of 6px). More legible at YouTube 1080p+, more visually prominent on the recording.
